Figure 1: Phylogenetic tree with per-cluster fold-preference heatmap.

The pair's MSA as a phylogenetic tree, one leaf per sequence cluster. Leaf rings show each cluster's predicted fold preference; the heatmap gives the seven analysed methods (AF2/AF3/ESMFold2/Boltz-2/BioEmu/ΔΔG/S4PRED) × clusters, followed by the two contact predictors (MSA-Transformer/CCMpred), which are shown for reference and enter no analysis; internal-node markers flag parsimony-inferred gain/loss/swap of fold state. Those markers come from four-state calls derived from the structure predictions, not from observations. The core question: do different groups of related sequences predict different folds? Clusters are tree-guided groups, not clades: only 12% are monophyletic across the dataset.
