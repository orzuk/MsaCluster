PURPOSE
-------
MsaCluster studies fold-switching proteins -- homologous proteins that adopt
different 3D structures. It builds deep MSAs seeded by protein pairs with
different folds, clusters them into sub-alignments, predicts structures per
cluster (AF2/AF3/ESMFold/ESM3/Boltz-2/AlphaFlow), evaluates contact maps
(MSA-Transformer, CCMpred), builds phylogenetic trees with ancestral
reconstruction, computes energetics (ThermoMPNN-based DDG and PyRosetta
Delta-G), and runs cross-method concordance analysis with sequence-divergence
correction + BH FDR control on the resulting candidate clade-level
fold-switching families.

DATASET: 93 protein pairs (full) or 10 pairs (test).
FORMAT:  <pdb1><chain>_<pdb2><chain>  e.g. "1dzlA_5keqF"

Controls: 100 IDP (Porter & Looger 2022), 48 Pfam-matched single-fold,
          101 random single-chain PDB. Total 249 controls.
