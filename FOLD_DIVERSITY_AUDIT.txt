Context: Phase 10 (ancestral reconstruction) and the fold-diversity survey
(scripts/fold_diversity_survey.py) need three per-pair CSVs:
  - df_af.csv   (AF2 TM-scores per cluster)
  - df_esm.csv  (ESMFold TM-scores per cluster, 10 samples each)

then the ancestral reconstruction maps those labels onto the phylogenetic tree.

transitions). The question was: is this a real biological result, or is it
because most of the data is missing? Answer: mostly missing data.

================================================================================
5. PIPELINE STAGE REFERENCE
================================================================================

How each CSV is generated:

  df_af.csv:
    Raw input:  AF_preds/*.pdb[.gz] (5 ranked models per cluster)
    Generator:  postprocess_unified.py -> _read_or_compute_af()
                -> AF_analysis.py -> compute_AF_pred_tmscores()
    Trigger:    --run_mode postprocess
    Output:     Pipeline/FoldPairs/<pair>/Analysis/df_af.csv

  df_esm.csv:
    Raw input:  output_esm_fold/esm2/*_esm2.pdb (10 samples per cluster)
    Generator:  postprocess_unified.py -> _read_or_compute_esm()
                -> esmfold_analysis.py -> compute_df_esm()
    Trigger:    --run_mode postprocess
    Output:     Pipeline/FoldPairs/<pair>/Analysis/df_esm.csv

  df_cmap.csv:
    Raw input:  output_cmaps/msa_transformer/msa_t__*.npy
    Also needs: _seed_both.a3m (pairwise alignment of two fold sequences)
    Generator:  postprocess_unified.py -> _read_or_compute_cmap()
                -> cmap_analysis.py -> compute_cmap_metrics_for_pair()
    Trigger:    --run_mode postprocess
    Output:     Pipeline/FoldPairs/<pair>/Analysis/df_cmap.csv

Caching: postprocess skips pairs that already have CSVs unless
--force_rerun_postprocess TRUE is passed.

================================================================================
6. EXPECTED OUTCOME
================================================================================

After all steps complete, the fold diversity survey will have data from
3 independent methods for ~86-93 pairs. The key scientific question:

  "Which method (AF2, ESMFold, MSA-Transformer) shows the most fold
   differentiation across clusters?"

Literature predicts: MSAT > ESM > AF2 (because AF2 memorizes training
structures rather than reading co-evolutionary signal from the MSA).

If MSAT shows clusters with different fold preferences but AF2 doesn't,
that supports doing ancestral reconstruction with MSAT labels (contact
map recall) rather than AF2 TM-scores.

See ancestral_reconstruction_issues.txt Sections 6-7 for full method
assessment and the multi-method comparison plan.
